Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Bellcourt Homes ("we", "us", "our") provides cross-border property advisory services to resident Indian investors purchasing residential property in London, UK. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, how it is used and shared, and how you can control it.

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry, request our LRS & TCS Roadmap, or book a discovery call, we collect:

  • Your full name
  • Phone / WhatsApp number
  • Email address
  • Any details you share with us directly during calls or messages, such as investment budget, timeline, and property preferences

We also use standard website analytics and advertising cookies (including Meta Pixel) to measure how visitors interact with this site — see the Cookies section below.

Why we collect it

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and get in touch via phone, WhatsApp, or email
  • Send the LRS & TCS Roadmap or other materials you've requested
  • Qualify your investment goals, LRS headroom, and timeline ahead of a discovery call
  • Coordinate your property search and purchase process with our London-based partner
  • Improve our marketing and better understand which channels bring us genuine enquiries

Who we share it with

To deliver the service, your details may be shared with:

  • Our London partner, who manages developer relationships, property walkthroughs, and solicitor coordination for your purchase
  • UK developers and solicitors, only once you've indicated genuine interest in proceeding with a specific property
  • Service providers who support our operations (e.g. our CRM/lead-tracking tools, WhatsApp Business)

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not share it with anyone outside this process without telling you.

How long we keep it

We retain your information for as long as you remain an active or prospective client, and for a reasonable period afterward in case you return or for our own record-keeping and legal obligations. If you ask us to delete your data and you are not in an active transaction, we will do so within a reasonable timeframe.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel, to understand site traffic and measure the effectiveness of our advertising. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time; doing so may affect how the site functions.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your information, where we're not required to keep it for legal or legitimate business reasons
  • Stop contacting you — just let us know via WhatsApp, phone, or email and we'll action it promptly

Not investment, legal, or tax advice

Bellcourt Homes facilitates introductions between property buyers and UK developers/solicitors and does not provide investment, legal, or tax advice. Any figures shared with you are illustrative. Please consult a qualified CA and solicitor before making any investment decision.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our services or applicable regulations evolve. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
